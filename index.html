In short

Parked keeps everything on your phone. We run no servers, and no data about you ever reaches us. The one thing that leaves your phone is the location of a saved parking spot, which is sent to your phone's geocoding service (Google's, on most Android phones) to look up its street address.

What the app handles, and why

Location. When the Bluetooth connection to a device you have marked as your car disconnects, Parked records your phone's location, including when the app is closed or not in use. That is the app's only feature: showing you where you parked and navigating you back. Location is read for up to 60 seconds after the disconnect and at no other time. Parked gets the position from Google Play services on your phone; how Google Play services itself handles location is set by your phone's location settings and Google's privacy policy.

Bluetooth devices. To let you choose your car, the app lists the devices paired with your phone. For the ones you choose, it stores the device name, its hardware (MAC) address, any name you give it, and whether you want a notification for it. When you turn on accurate mode, Android records an association between Parked and that device. Android keeps that record, and you can remove it in your phone's settings.

Parking history. The app keeps the last five parking spots for each device, each with its coordinates, accuracy, time and street address.

Photos. The Photo button in the parking notification opens your camera app. Photos are taken and stored by the camera app, and Parked never sees them.

Purchases. If you buy Parked Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive a confirmation that the purchase exists, never your payment details.

What leaves your phone

  • Street address lookup. The coordinates of each saved spot are sent to the geocoding service built into your phone — Google's, on most Android phones — which returns the street address. It is subject to that provider's privacy policy.
  • Google Play, for purchases, as described above.
  • Android backup. If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include Parked's settings (your chosen devices, their names, and your preferred maps app) in it, so they return when you set up a new phone. Parking history is deliberately excluded.

That is all. Parked contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it has no user accounts. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Each device keeps its five most recent parking spots; older ones are deleted automatically as new ones arrive. To delete everything, clear the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Parked → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app. Since we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy policy.

Security

Parking history and settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Parked itself opens no network connections: the address lookup and purchases are carried out by Google Play services on your phone.

Children

Parked is intended for adults and is not directed at children.
